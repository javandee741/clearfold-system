# ADR-0002: Documentation language policy

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-01
- Affects: specifications, website, translations

## Decision

- v0.15 Russian documents remain the historical normative baseline until reviewed English equivalents are frozen.
- From v0.16 onward, English is the target canonical language for normative specifications.
- Russian and Simplified Chinese are Tier-1 full-document translations.
- Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Spanish are Tier-2 languages: landing pages, README and high-value documents first.
- Portuguese (Brazil), German and French are Tier-3 community-driven translations unless demand changes.

Translations must record the source document/version they correspond to and must not silently diverge in normative meaning.
